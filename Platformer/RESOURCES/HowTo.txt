Welcome to Generic Arena Platformer (or G.A.P.)!

Move around using the left and right arrow keys.

Jump using the 'S' key. Hold the key to jump higher.
Press 'S' in the air to double jump.

While in the air, press the down arrow key to dive.

Shoot a single-damage bullet using the 'D' key.

Dash using the 'W' key. You are invulnerable while dashing.
Dash through your enemies for triple damage.

Press 'F1' for extra debug info.
Double tap  the 'Escape' key to quit.

NOTE : the mentioned keybinds are configurable in the menu.


Enemies will spawn in the arena in waves.
Survive each wave of enemies to win the game.

You start with 5 health. Each killed enemy will heal you
for 1 damage, but not past your health limit - 10 HP.

Have fun!
